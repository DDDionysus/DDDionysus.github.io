About

I am interested in building and understanding machine learning systems for visual data.

I am an MSc Applied Computational Science and Engineering graduate from Imperial College London, with a BSc in Computer Science from the University of Bristol.

My interests lie in computer vision, self-supervised representation learning, robust machine learning, multimodal learning and scalable ML systems. My recent research investigates the adversarial robustness of self-supervised vision transformers, particularly DINOv2 and DINOv3.

MSc Individual Research Project · Imperial College London · 2026

Evaluating the Robustness of Self-Supervised Vision Transformers to Adversarial Perturbations

A Study of Gram Anchoring in DINOv3

A comparative study of DINOv2 and DINOv3 under adversarial perturbations, investigating robustness at both representation and downstream-task levels. The work examines CLS and patch-token behaviour under FGSM and PGD attacks, alongside the role of Gram Anchoring in DINOv3.
